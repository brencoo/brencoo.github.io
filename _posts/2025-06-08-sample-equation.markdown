Here’s a post demonstrating how to include a math equation in a Jekyll blog using MathJax.

You can write inline equations like this: \( E = mc^2 \)

Or display equations like this:

$$
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2} \, dx = \sqrt{\pi}
$$

Make sure you include MathJax support in your layout or `head.html` for this to render properly.
