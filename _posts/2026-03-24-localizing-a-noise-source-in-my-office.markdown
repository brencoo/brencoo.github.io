When I sit in my office, depending on the angle my head is tilted I can hear a very high-pitched tone. It does not bother me, but I was surprised how well I could locate its origin by turning my head around. Even a turn of 5-10 degrees already yielded a massive difference to the point that I could barely hear it anymore!

To verify my ears, I downloaded a decibel meter to my phone, formed a cup around the microphone with my hands and started pointing. Voilà, ~24.9dB at around 13.3kHz when I point it at the source and at a slight angle only ~16.4dB.
